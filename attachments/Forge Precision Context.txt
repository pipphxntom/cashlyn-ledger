## WHAT WE DO

Forge Precision is a **B2B receivables and order-to-cash (O2C) consultancy** that helps mid-market companies find and free cash trapped in overdue receivables.

We are NOT a debt collection agency. We are NOT a software company. We are a specialized advisory and managed-service firm that:

1. **Diagnoses** — Maps the full invoice-to-cash process, segments the AR aging by root cause, and quantifies exactly how much cash is stuck and why
2. **Fixes** — Redesigns dunning workflows, escalation paths, credit terms, dispute resolution, and collections prioritization
3. **Automates** — Deploys AI-powered dunning sequences, cash-application helpers, aging dashboards, and collections prioritization models
4. **Manages** — Optionally runs the optimized collections process on a monthly retainer, acting as an outsourced AR intelligence layer

**The core value proposition:** Mid-market companies typically carry 15–25 extra days of DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) from process gaps, not bad customers. Forge Precision identifies those gaps, quantifies them in rupees, and closes them — freeing working capital without changing the customer base or revenue.

## COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

### Where Forge Precision sits in the market

**Enterprise BPO/Advisory (EXL, Genpact, Accenture, Cognizant):** These firms chase Fortune 500 accounts with large teams and high minimums. They will not serve a ₹200Cr Indian manufacturer. Forge Precision operates in the gap they leave behind.

**Software vendors (HighRadius, Billtrust, Tesorio, Paraglide):** These sell AR automation platforms. They are tools, not operators. Most mid-market companies buy the software and use 30% of it because they lack the process expertise to implement it properly. Forge Precision makes these tools deliver ROI — we're implementation partners, not competitors.

**India-based AR outsourcing (Invensis, smaller BPOs):** These provide headcount — bodies to make collections calls. Forge Precision provides strategy, diagnosis, and AI-augmented operations. Different value proposition entirely.

**Solo CA firms / freelance consultants:** Generalists who occasionally touch AR as part of broader financial advisory. Forge Precision is the specialist — depth over breadth.

**Forge Precision's wedge:** The person who understands O2C process AND automation, serving mid-market companies the giants ignore, deploying tools the software vendors sell but don't operationalize.

## AI & AUTOMATION ADVANTAGE (THE "POLSIA MODEL")

Forge Precision operates as a **one-person firm with AI as the back office.** Inspired by companies like Polsia (where one person runs an entire business using AI agents for 80% of operations), the founder uses AI to replace the team that would normally sit behind a consultant:

| Function | AI handles | Founder handles |
|---|---|---|
| AR aging analysis | Claude + Python: automated ingestion, segmentation, visualization | Review, interpretation, strategic commentary |
| DSO benchmarking | Automated benchmark lookup and comparison | Client-specific context and recommendations |
| Dunning sequences | AI-generated, human-approved email templates | Strategy, escalation decisions, relationship management |
| Client reports | Auto-generated weekly dashboards from data | Strategic narrative, action items, client communication |
| Proposals & presentations | Claude drafts from discovery call notes | Customization, delivery, relationship |
| Content marketing | Claude assists with drafts | Voice, perspective, publishing |
| Collections prioritization | Scoring models auto-rank call lists | Exception handling, judgment calls |

**Proprietary frameworks:**
- **CLEVER 3.0:** Token optimization framework that allows running agentic AI workflows at significantly reduced cost — enabling deeper analysis per client at better margins
- **ARGUS architecture:** Multi-agent system design experience applicable to building automated collections workflows for clients

## BRAND VOICE & COMMUNICATION STYLE

**Tone:** Direct, confident, no-nonsense. Speaks in rupees and days, not jargon. Uses the CFO's language (cash flow, working capital, DSO, bad debt) rather than consultant-speak (optimization, synergy, transformation).

**Core messaging principles:**
1. Lead with the number, not the methodology — "₹4 crore is stuck" beats "we optimize your O2C process"
2. The client's problem is always stated in their language, never ours
3. Specificity over generality — "28% of your overdue traces to wrong billing contacts" beats "we improve collections efficiency"
4. Honesty over polish — if we can't help, we say so; if the timeline is aggressive, we say so
5. Results are quantified or not mentioned — no unearned claims

**Key phrases / positioning lines:**
- "We find the cash your receivables are hiding."
- "AI answers questions. We ask the right questions."
- "You have ₹[X] crore stuck beyond terms. We get it back."
- "The expensive part isn't the tool. It's knowing what to look for."
- "We help mid-market finance teams free trapped working capital — without changing your customer base."

**Phrases we NEVER use:**
- "Synergy," "leverage" (as a verb), "paradigm shift," "holistic solution"
- "World-class," "best-in-class," "cutting-edge" (unearned superlatives)
- "We are passionate about..." (nobody cares about our passion; they care about their cash)
- "Debt collection" (we are advisory, not collectors)
